Making it your firm's own

The package is built to be adapted, and five questions do most of the tailoring:

  1. Who would serve as the firm's AI Policy Administrator.
  2. The practice areas, and the counties where the firm regularly files.
  3. What AI tools people are actually using today, and on what accounts. The honest inventory matters more than the aspirational one.
  4. Whether any clients impose AI restrictions in engagement terms or outside counsel guidelines.
  5. The firm's appetite on voluntary disclosure: the CMC attachment as a default in every litigated matter, or reserved for matters where AI use is material.

None of this is meant to be absorbed in one sitting. If a document raises a question, or you want the presentation live for your partners, write me or call.
